## What it is

At Dopamina, I lead the engineering behind an AI-powered marketing workspace. Ergo routes WhatsApp requests across Content Studio, Publishing, Community and Ads, keeping human direction at the center.

## How a message becomes marketing

1. **WhatsApp** — the request arrives as a message.
2. **Doppies** — AI agents pick it up.
3. **Content Studio**
4. **Publishing**
5. **Community**
6. **Ads**

AI agents, plus your direction: the system coordinates the work, and a person stays in charge of it.
